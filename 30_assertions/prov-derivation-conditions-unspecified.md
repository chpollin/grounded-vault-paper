# PROV asserts derivation without defining its conditions

## Statement

PROV-DM defines derivation as a transformation of an entity into another, an update of an entity resulting in a new one, or the construction of a new entity based on a pre-existing entity. It then states explicitly that PROV does not attempt to specify the conditions under which derivations exist, and that derivation is considered to have been determined by unspecified means. The model does name a necessary ingredient without turning it into a criterion, since a chain of usage and generation is necessary but not sufficient for a derivation to hold, some form of influence occurring during the activities involved being needed as well. The relation therefore records that an influence took place and leaves the determination of that influence outside the model.
